### Objective
Learn to give and receive feedback about games.

### Method
Review and respond to reviews of computer games


## Requirements 
Part one, play the game of another group then give them feedback about your experience. Part two, having received feedback from at least one student from another group, provide a response indicating what you/your group will do based on the feedback. 
The feedback and response should be documented in an **issue in git**.   The feedback should be in the description of the issue and the response should be given as a comment. This will also allow you to use smart commits to connect any code changes with the feedback.

## Documentation:
Each student will submit both a the feedback they gave and the response they constructed to feedback.  This will include two links, one to the issue for the feedback you gave, and one to the issue with your response.
